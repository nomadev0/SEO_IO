# SEO Rulebook (high impact)

## Critical
- Noindex accidental in indexable templates.
- HTTP 4xx/5xx responses on internal links or sitemap entries.
- Mixed protocol (HTTPS downgrade) on canonical templates.



## High
- Missing or conflicting canonical.
- robots.txt blocking URLs with search demand.
- Sitemap inaccessible, stale or full of soft 404.
- Hreflang broken or incomplete.
- Redirect chains longer than one hop.
- Core Web Vitals (LCP/INP/CLS) above target per template.

## Medium
- Title and H1 missing, duplicated or out of range.
- Meta description missing or outside 120-165 characters.
- Key images without descriptive alt.
- Open Graph / Twitter basic tags missing.
- Internal links broken or pointing to temporary redirects.
- Canonical pointing to non-indexable location.
- Excessive internal nofollow usage.

## Low
- Heading hierarchy skipping levels.
- Thin content (less than 300 words) on strategic templates.
- Missing lang attribute on html.
- No favicon or manifest on priority templates.
